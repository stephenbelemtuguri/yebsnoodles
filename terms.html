Orders

Orders are confirmed only after YEBS accepts them. Prices, availability, preparation time, and delivery eligibility can change. Confirmed order details shown in the app are the applicable order record.

Payment and delivery

Mobile Money payments must be authorised by the account holder. Cash payments are accepted only when the option is displayed. Delivery fees and times depend on address, distance, weather, traffic, and rider availability.

Cancellations and refunds

Cancellation requests must be submitted before fulfilment reaches a restricted stage. Refunds require admin review. Approved refunds may be reduced by disclosed fees and returned through a supported payment method.

Customer responsibilities

Customers must provide accurate contact and delivery information, receive orders at the nominated location, and use the service lawfully. Abuse, fraudulent payment evidence, account sharing, and unsafe conduct may result in suspension.

Support

Questions and complaints should be submitted through the customer support link or the order tracking page. Delivery safety and payment disputes should be reported promptly with the relevant order number.
