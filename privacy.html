Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

YEBS Noodles collects the information needed to accept and deliver orders, including your name, phone number, delivery address or pin, order contents, payment references, and customer support messages.

How information is used

We use personal data to process orders, calculate delivery, dispatch drivers, prevent payment abuse, provide receipts and customer support, and improve the service. Location data is used for delivery and live tracking while an eligible order is active.

Payments and notifications

Mobile Money payment requests are processed by authorised payment providers. Payment references may be retained for reconciliation. WhatsApp, SMS, email, or in-app notifications are sent only for account, order, or service purposes where configured.

Retention and security

Operational records are retained for order fulfilment, accounting, fraud prevention, and legal obligations. Access is restricted by account role and protected by authentication controls.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, export, or deletion of personal data by contacting YEBS through WhatsApp or by using the support details on this website. We may retain records where required for payment, tax, fraud, or legal compliance.
